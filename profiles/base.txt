# Universal safe list - applies to every Android TV.
# Format: package | risk | reason
# "safe" = disabling breaks nothing you interact with on a TV.
# Everything here is disabled with pm disable-user (reversible), never uninstalled.

com.google.android.tvrecommendations | safe | Google TV recommendation row on the home screen
com.google.android.leanbacklauncher.recommendations | safe | Google Shop / app recommendation row
com.google.android.feedback | safe | crash and usage reporting
com.google.android.onetimeinitializer | safe | one-time setup initializer
com.google.android.partnersetup | safe | carrier / partner setup wizard
com.google.android.syncadapters.calendar | safe | calendar sync adapter
com.android.printspooler | safe | print spooler, unused on a TV
com.android.dreams.basic | safe | AOSP screensaver (the launcher provides a clock instead)
com.android.egg | safe | easter egg
com.android.providers.calendar | safe | calendar provider
com.android.providers.contacts | safe | contacts provider
com.android.providers.userdictionary | safe | user dictionary provider
com.android.adservices.api | safe | advertising ID service
com.google.android.apps.mediashell | safe | local media playback shell
